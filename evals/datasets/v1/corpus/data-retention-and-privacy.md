# Data retention and privacy

## Log retention
Request logs are kept for 3 days on Free, 7 days on Pro, 30 days on Team and 90 days on Enterprise.

## Deleting data
When a customer deletes a project, its data is removed from production systems immediately and purged from backups within 35 days. A deleted workspace cannot be restored after 14 days.

## Data location
Customers choose a data region when they create a workspace: US East in Virginia or EU West in Frankfurt. The region cannot be changed afterwards. Moving to another region requires creating a new workspace and migrating the data.

## Security
Data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and with TLS 1.2 or later in transit. Helio holds a SOC 2 Type II report, which Team and Enterprise customers can request. A data processing agreement for GDPR is available on every paid plan.
