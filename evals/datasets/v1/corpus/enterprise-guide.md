# Enterprise guide

## Contracts
Enterprise agreements run for a minimum of 12 months and are renewed by mutual agreement. Pricing, limits and support terms are listed in the order form that is signed with the contract.

## Single sign-on setup
Single sign-on is configured with the identity provider's SAML metadata. After the configuration is tested, an owner can require sign-in through the identity provider for all members.

## Dedicated support
Every Enterprise customer is assigned a technical account manager who joins a monthly review call.
