# Billing and refunds

## Invoices and payment
Invoices are issued on the first day of every month and are due within 14 days. Customers can pay by credit card on every plan. Payment by ACH bank transfer is available on Team and Enterprise only. Enterprise customers can also pay by wire transfer against a purchase order.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, Helio retries the charge 3 times over 7 days. After the last failed attempt the workspace is downgraded to the Free plan, and projects above the Free limit become read-only. No data is deleted during the downgrade.

## Changing plans
Upgrades take effect immediately and the difference is charged pro rata. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Refunds
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of the first purchase. Monthly plans are not refundable, but cancelling stops the next invoice. Refund requests are sent to the billing team and are processed within 10 business days to the original payment method.

## Taxes
Prices exclude VAT and sales tax. Customers in the European Union who provide a valid VAT number are not charged VAT.
