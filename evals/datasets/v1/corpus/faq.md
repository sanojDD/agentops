# Frequently asked questions

## Can I change my plan in the middle of the month?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade from the billing page at any time, and the change is applied according to the billing rules.

## Where is my data stored?
Your data is stored in the data region that was selected when the workspace was created.

## Do you offer a free tier?
Yes, the Free plan is available without a credit card and has no time limit.

## How do I get help?
Use the support channel that belongs to your plan. The community forum is open to everyone.

## Can I use Helio for a hobby project?
Yes. The Free plan is intended for evaluation and personal projects.
