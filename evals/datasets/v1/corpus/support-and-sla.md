# Support and service levels

## Support channels
The Free plan includes access to the community forum only. Pro customers receive email support with a first response within 2 business days. Team customers receive a first response within 1 business day. Enterprise customers have a dedicated channel with 24/7 coverage.

## Priority levels
A P1 issue means production is down for all users. The first response target for a P1 issue is 1 hour on Enterprise and 4 hours on Team. P2 issues degrade a feature, and P3 issues are questions or minor problems.

## Uptime commitment
Team workspaces are covered by a 99.9 percent monthly uptime SLA and Enterprise workspaces by 99.99 percent. If monthly uptime falls below the SLA but stays above 99.0 percent, the customer receives a credit of 10 percent of that month's fee. Below 99.0 percent the credit is 25 percent. Credits must be requested within 30 days.

## Support hours
Business days for email support are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in the customer's region.
