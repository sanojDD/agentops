# Incidents and maintenance

## Status page
Current incidents and past uptime are published on the Helio status page. Customers can subscribe to updates by email or webhook.

## Scheduled maintenance
Planned maintenance is announced at least 7 days in advance. Maintenance windows are on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC. Maintenance that needs downtime is limited to 30 minutes per window.

## Incident severity
A P1 incident is a full outage, a P2 incident is a partial outage or a serious slowdown, and a P3 incident is a minor issue with a workaround.

## Postmortems
After every P1 incident Helio publishes a postmortem within 5 business days. The postmortem describes the timeline, the root cause and the actions taken to prevent a repeat.

## Reporting a problem
Customers can report suspected incidents through the support channel of their plan. Reports about a security vulnerability should be sent to the security team and are acknowledged within 2 business days.
