# Plans and pricing

Helio Cloud is available in four plans: Free, Pro, Team and Enterprise.

## Free
The Free plan costs nothing and includes 3 projects and 1 workspace member. It is meant for evaluation and small personal projects. API requests on the Free plan are limited to 60 requests per minute.

## Pro
The Pro plan costs 29 dollars per month per workspace and includes 20 projects. Pro workspaces can have up to 3 members. Pro customers receive email support.

## Team
The Team plan costs 99 dollars per month and includes unlimited projects and up to 10 members. Additional members can be added for 12 dollars per member per month. Team workspaces get audit logs, ACH payments and a 99.9 percent uptime SLA.

## Enterprise
Enterprise pricing is custom and is agreed in a signed contract. Enterprise includes single sign-on with SAML, a dedicated support channel and a 99.99 percent uptime SLA.

## Discounts and trials
Paying annually instead of monthly gives a 20 percent discount on Pro and Team. New Pro and Team workspaces start with a 14-day free trial that does not require a credit card. Non-profit organizations can apply for a 50 percent discount on Team.
