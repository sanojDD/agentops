Acme Co. Customer Policy Handbook

Refund policy
Customers may return any product within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. The product must be unused and in its original packaging. Enterprise customers with a signed contract receive an extended return window of 60 days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned item is received.

Shipping
Standard shipping takes 5 business days within the country. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs an additional 15 dollars. International orders are shipped by air freight and usually arrive within 14 business days. Customers receive a tracking number by email once the order has shipped.

Support hours
The support team is available Monday to Friday, from 9 AM to 6 PM local time. Urgent issues for enterprise customers can be reported to the priority support line at any time, including weekends. Standard support tickets receive a first response within 24 hours.

Warranty
All hardware products include a one year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects. Accidental damage and water damage are not covered. Warranty claims require the original proof of purchase.
